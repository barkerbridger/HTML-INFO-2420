Web Site Critiques

Review Questions

  1. What was your first impression of this site?
  2. How does this site organize the flow of information? Do they have links on the left and text on the right? Some other scheme? No scheme?
  3. Does the site structure make sense or does it confuse you? Why?
  4. Does the site graphics/layout/multimedia enhance the site or detract from it? Why?
  5. How long did it take for the home page to load? Was the load time reasonable?
  6. Was the text color and size readable? If a background color or image was used, did it enhance or detract from the site?
  7. Did the site have a navigation bar or other navigation tool? Was the navigation well done?
  8. Did the site have a color scheme or look and feel that was repeated on all the pages?
  9. How does the site respond to smaller window sizes (responsive design)?
  10. What does this site offer that was unusual or very well done?

Pitchfork - http://pitchfork.com

I really liked the design and found the site to be fluid and easy to navigate.

The pitchfork site is split in sections, inside each section is more columnns varying from 2 to 3 sections. Some sections are split with columns and rows as well. It has a header that gives you various options to choose from

The website is easy to use, however I will say there is a lot of information and a tad bit overwhelming. Every single article or section as a image or graphic associated with it making it pleasant to look at.

Overall the layout is nice, I would say the use of graphics enhances it greatly and makes the user more likely to interact with the content. Like I mentioned previously the use of moving images may be distracting/overwhelming.

It didn't take long at all. I found it to be reasonable. I have a good computer so maybe slower or limited processing computers may have issues.

Text and color are readable, no background detractions in my opinion, just the content itself.

Yes the top of the page has navigation bars and some sections with a lot of information have a "see more" or next selection.

The scheme is repeated. The website seems to be article and content focused throughout all options in the navigation bar.

Pitchfork.com adjusts very well, when it is smaller it will make the site one column and fluidly scrolls.

Overall I think this web page is very well done. I consider myself tech savvy and nothing about it was difficult to use. I was very appealing and the aricles or clickable content was well organized.
